# deskOrganiser
This is a thoughtfully designed desk organiser with a charging station for your phone, especially iphone, if you keep it here while charging your iphone goes into standby mode. It also has different sections for different types of items. I personally had cards, earpods, pens, pencils in my mind when i made this apart from iphone's standby mode. The design is compact, doesn't waste filament on support. The size is perfect if you have a small desk in your room and also use laptop or do other things on the same table.

# Why I made this?
I made this because small desks quickly fills up and becomes a mess with all your stuffs randomly spread around. I wanted an organiser that keeps my earpods, keys, cards, coins, and other small stuffs together in one place without occupying much space.
